# Five short examples, not required answers

All five use the fictional advisory-firm case. These are prepared examples, not actual participant responses. Keep the full brief's six sections when writing your table's output.

## Research

**Conditional finding:** measured benefits in customer support do not establish gains in advisory work. METR's different result prompts us to measure total completion time. **Evidence:** E1 and E2 in the source cards, with their settings and dates. **Challenge:** no direct firm-specific evidence. **Next question:** which tasks resemble the measured studies? **Possible learning:** a repeatable verification process may matter as much as drafting ability.

## Simulation

**Inputs:** 120 analyst hours, 12 reviewer hours, demand 20; eight analyst hours and one review hour per brief today. **Baseline:** 12 completed briefs. **Agent assumptions:** four analyst hours and 1.5 review hours -> eight briefs. **One-input change:** review 0.75 -> 16 briefs. **Challenge:** equal quality is untested. **Next measurement:** time and defects through the complete workflow.

## Role playing

**Decision:** pilot a smaller-client brief service? **Conflict:** leader wants more customers; reviewer fears overload; customer wants trustworthy results; analyst wants support to learn. **Changed condition:** source traceability is required. **Revision:** narrow the pilot and include a source packet. **Unresolved issue:** who pays for review? All reactions are invented hypotheses. **Real question:** what verification would a prospective client need?

## Forecasting

**Faster change:** by 2030, traceable drafts and reliable review support a new service. **Slower change:** review time and weak client trust keep use internal. **Separating assumption:** review can scale at stable quality. **Signpost:** during a documented pilot, measure completed briefs and defects monthly. A suggested threshold is more than 12 weekly briefs without worse quality; this is a fictional table target, not an industry standard. **Robust action:** teach source verification in either future.

## Problem solving

**Obstacle:** review limits the assumed workflow. **Responses:** better source packets, narrower briefs, or retain current service. **Constraint:** no extra reviewer time. **Chosen trial:** compare a few paired public-information briefs with and without source packets; record total preparation and review time and use the same quality checklist. **Responsible role:** pilot lead. **Continue only if:** quality does not worsen and total workload improves under criteria agreed before the trial. **Unknown:** whether the small comparison generalizes. This is a test plan, not a successful test.
