# What could change in an industry you know?

**You talk. The student helps run Claude Code. Together, you judge the work.**

1. **Pick an industry.** Choose one someone at the table knows.
2. **Pick one change.** Finish: “AI agents might make it possible to ___.” Say what you expect before you begin.
3. **Choose one way to explore it.** Pick from the five options below. You can switch as your questions change.
4. **Try it for 20 minutes.** Ask questions. Challenge the answers. Add your experience.
5. **Test it.** What would make this fail? Who might resist? What are we assuming?
6. **Write one page together.** What did you find? What could happen? What must people still do? What changed in your thinking?

| Choose this | To ask this | Do this together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Is it happening? | Check examples and contrary evidence. |
| Simulation | What changes? | Change one assumption and compare results. |
| Role playing | Who sees it differently? | Take roles, disagree, then revise the idea. |
| Forecasting | What could make change faster or slower? | Compare two futures; name signs to watch. |
| Problem solving | What could stop this from working? | Try responses, add a constraint, design a small test. |

**Stuck?** Try this: Could a small advisory firm use agents to prepare more client briefs? The student has a ready-made case.

**Keep asking:** “What did the agent miss?” “What would change our minds?”

Use public examples. Made-up numbers and imagined reactions are starting points to test.

Your brief will inform the discussion about preparing students for these possible futures.

**Report back:** “We explored ___. We were surprised by ___. Our biggest uncertainty is ___.”
